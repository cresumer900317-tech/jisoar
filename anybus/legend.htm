진단

스타일: 내역: 예시:
왼쪽코너위 깜박거림 Station is active as a device, but also the Ext.Diag bit is active. This can indicate a critical problem on the attached I/O, measurement or control (e.g. short circuit, limit exceeded etc.) 10
오른쪽코너위 깜박거림 Station is active as a device, but also the Static.Diag bit is active. With this static bit the device indicates that no I/O is updated at this moment or that I/O or other measurement value is not (yet) ready to be transferred (startup phase of a DP/DP-V1 device). 11
양쪽코너위 깜박거림 기기내 진단데이터와 진단요청이 모두 존재함 (위설명 참조) 12
